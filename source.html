Open by design

Yours to play.
Yours to build on.

The games are and will remain free, open source, ad-free, and playable without an account. All code, drawings, and synthesized sounds in this collection are shared under the MIT license.

Curious how a maze is made? Want to give your snake a new color? These are small games with readable source. Explore the files, learn something, make them your own.

A small shared foundation

Input · Sound · Game controls · Shared styles

Contributions are welcome, especially fixes, accessibility improvements, and careful playtesting. Report an issue or contribute a pull request. The project README explains how to run, test, and contribute.

Your play stays yours

No analytics, cookies, or outside services are used by the games. Sound preference, difficulty, and best scores are saved only in your browser. Clear your browser’s site data to reset them.
